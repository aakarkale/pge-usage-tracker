🧾 When is your billing cycle?

PG&E bills on a roughly monthly cycle that usually doesn't start on the 1st. Enter the start and end dates of one recent cycle (from any PG&E bill) and Wattwise will align every chart and project your current bill. You can skip this and set it later.
